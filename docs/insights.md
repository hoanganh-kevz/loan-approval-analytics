\# Key Insights



\## 1. Loan Approval Overview



The analysis evaluates loan applications based on approval status, credit score, financial characteristics, employment status, loan purpose, and risk score.



Key metrics include:



\- Total loan applications

\- Approved loans

\- Approval rate

\- Average credit score

\- Average risk score



\## 2. Credit Score and Approval



Loan approval rates vary across different credit score ranges.



The analysis divides applicants into four credit score groups:



\- Below 580

\- 580–669

\- 670–739

\- 740+



This helps identify the relationship between credit quality and loan approval.



\## 3. Employment Status



Approval behavior and financial characteristics differ across:



\- Employed

\- Self-Employed

\- Unemployed



Debt-to-income ratio and loan amount were compared across employment groups.



\## 4. Loan Purpose



Loan applications were analyzed by purpose to identify differences in:



\- Number of applications

\- Approval rate

\- Average loan amount

\- Loan amount relative to annual income



\## 5. Risk Analysis



Applicants were segmented according to RiskScore.



The analysis compares:



\- Approval rate

\- Average loan amount

\- Average credit score

\- Interest rate



This provides a clearer view of borrower risk segments.



\## 6. Age Analysis



Applicants were divided into four age groups:



\- Below 25

\- 25–40

\- 41–60

\- Above 60



RiskScore and application volume were compared across these groups.



\## 7. Business Value



The analysis can help financial institutions:



\- Identify borrower segments with different approval probabilities

\- Understand the relationship between credit quality and approval

\- Monitor risk across borrower groups

\- Support data-driven loan approval decisions
